This app provides a simple comparison of three different learning with rate curve (LRC) models implemented in ACEIT 8.2: Unit Theory, Cumulative Average (CUMAV) Direct, and CUMAV Iterative. Click here for more details.
  • Use the left sidebar to set the learning curve parameters.
  • Click in the bottom plot to set the quantity stream.
  • Click and drag in the top plot and use the buttons on the right to zoom in and out of a specific region.
Resizing or zooming of the browser window can cause plots to break. Click the Reset button to restore the plots.
